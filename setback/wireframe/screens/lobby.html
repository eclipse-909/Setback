Table Rules

Teams Rule Set Standard pairs seats across the table into two fixed teams. Custom lets the host assign each seat's team by hand.
Win By 2 The winning team must finish at least 2 points ahead. If they don't, play continues past the target score.
Negative Scores Teams can drop below zero if they're set back by more than they've earned.
Dealer Override If a player bids 4, the dealer may override the bid by bidding 4.
Play To The score a team must reach — and clear, if Win By 2 is on — to win the game.
