About the Game

Setback is an exciting card game to play with your friends or competitively online. Cooperate with teammates or play every man for himself to strategically defeat your opponents. Download now and put your skills to the test!

Features

  • Engaging Gameplay

    Come up with the best strategy to take down your opponents. Play the right card at the right time, and take control of the table. You don't have to let luck decide your fate!

  • Multiplayer With Friends

    Can't play Setback with your friends in person? No matter! Create a private lobby and invite your friends for a fun game of Setback!

  • Online Multiplayer

    Compete with players around the world and prove your skills in multiplayer mode.

  • Single Player

    Want to play a quick game by yourself? Just start up a game and play against the AI. Hone your skills for the next game!
